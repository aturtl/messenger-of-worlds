New Wierd Game Jam Dev Log

My devlog last time (GWJ #94) felt a little bit bland. I was just stating objectives, but I realize a devlog can be much more personal and creative than just that.
So I've decided I will not just list what I create, but my thoughts behind them as well.

7/26:
	- Created a basic plan (plan.txt). I think the idea's pretty bizarre.
	- Sketched out a basic dragonfly character.
		- I chose a dragonfly because of their spiritual symbolism (https://www.icysedgwick.com/dragonfly-folklore/)
		- Most notably inspired by the line: "In various spiritual pathways, the dragonfly acts as a messenger between the worlds.
		   They teach those who see them to ‘go with the flow’. But seeing one in your dreams is a warning." - Sedgwick
	- 3d-modeled and rigged dragonfly character
		- Took a bit longer than I wanted to since I hadn't used Blender for more than a year.
		- Attempted to use single planes for the wings but turns out Godot does not like those. (Frustration)
		   Tried to disable culling with shaders, but could not access original texture intuitively.
		   So, I ended up just extruding wings slightly.
	- Though I only had a couple hours to work with today, I intend to do a lot more tomorrow!
